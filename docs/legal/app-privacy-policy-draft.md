# SideCar App Privacy Policy — Publication Draft

Effective date: September 1, 2026

This is the publication draft for the SideCar mobile app operated by Amkai INC.
It must be approved by the account owner and replace the current waitlist-only
policy at `https://www.ride-sidecar.com/privacy` before the App Privacy label or
app version is submitted to Apple.

## Overview

SideCar helps verified college students find or post planned rides, coordinate
pickup and drop-off details, communicate with other riders and drivers, and
share trip costs. This policy explains the information SideCar collects, why it
is used, when it is shared, and the choices available to users.

## Information collected

Depending on how a user uses SideCar, the service may collect:

- Account and contact information, including name, email address, phone number,
  account identifiers, age, gender, language, and student-email verification.
- Profile and verification information, including a profile photo, identity
  verification status, driver's-license information, insurance documents,
  vehicle information, and review results. Identity verification may be
  completed through Stripe Identity.
- Ride and location information, including pickup and drop-off addresses,
  precise map coordinates selected for a ride, route information, trip dates
  and times, seats, luggage preferences, rider preferences, and live trip stop
  status when that feature is used.
- Transaction information, including ride price, fees, credits, refunds,
  payment status, payout status, and Stripe customer or connected-account
  identifiers. Payment card and bank-account details are collected and
  processed by Stripe and are not stored directly by SideCar.
- User content, including messages, reviews, ratings, support requests, safety
  reports, block lists, profile photos, and documents a user chooses to upload.
- Device and notification information, including device identifiers and push
  notification tokens used to deliver messages, booking updates, and trip
  reminders.
- Operational and security information needed to authenticate users, protect
  the service, prevent abuse, investigate disputes, and diagnose service
  failures.

## How information is used

SideCar uses information to:

- create and secure accounts and verify eligibility;
- operate rider and driver profiles;
- create, search, match, and manage rides and seat requests;
- display routes and coordinate pickup and drop-off locations;
- process payments, credits, refunds, and driver payouts;
- deliver chat messages, booking updates, and trip reminders;
- provide ratings, reviews, referrals, support, safety, block, and report tools;
- enforce service rules, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and protect users;
- comply with legal, accounting, and regulatory obligations; and
- maintain and improve the reliability of the service.

SideCar does not use the collected app data for cross-app advertising tracking.

## When information is shared

Information is shared only as needed to operate the service, including with:

- other users involved in a ride, such as profile, rating, vehicle, route,
  pickup, drop-off, and trip-coordination details;
- Stripe for payments, refunds, payouts, and identity verification;
- Google services for maps, places, route calculation, and sign-in when used;
- Firebase services for authentication, database, file storage, app integrity,
  configuration, cloud functions, and push notifications;
- service providers acting on SideCar's instructions;
- authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect
  users, the public, or the service; and
- a successor organization in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of
  assets, subject to applicable law.

SideCar does not sell personal information.

## Data retention and account deletion

SideCar keeps information only for as long as needed for the purposes described
above, including safety, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, and
legal obligations. Retention periods vary by data type and transaction status.

Users can request deletion from **Profile → Delete account**. Open rides,
pending bookings, refunds, or payouts must be completed or cancelled first.
After deletion, authentication data, profile details, uploaded user files, user
subcollections, and conversations are deleted or de-identified. Limited ride
and transaction records may be retained in de-identified or legally required
form to preserve trip history, financial records, safety records, and the rights
of other users.

## User choices

Users may:

- update profile information in the app;
- control location, camera, photo-library, and notification permissions through
  device settings;
- block or report another user;
- contact support about access, correction, deletion, or another privacy
  request; and
- delete their account in the app, subject to completion of open obligations.

## Children's privacy

SideCar is intended for users age 13 and older and for verified college-student
communities. The service is not directed to children under 13. If SideCar learns
that information from a child under 13 was collected, it will take appropriate
steps to delete it.

## Security and international processing

SideCar uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended
to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Information
may be processed in the United States and other locations where SideCar's
service providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the service, legal requirements, or data
practices change. The effective date will be updated, and material changes will
be communicated as required by law.

## Contact

Privacy and support questions can be sent to `hi@ride-sidecar.com` or through
the contact form at `https://www.ride-sidecar.com/#contact`.
