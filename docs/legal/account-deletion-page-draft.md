# Delete Your SideCar Account — Web Page Draft

This copy is intended for a public page such as
`https://www.ride-sidecar.com/delete-account`. It must be reviewed and deployed
before the Google Play Data safety form is completed.

## Delete your SideCar account and data

SideCar, operated by Amkai INC, lets users request account deletion directly in
the mobile app.

### Delete from the app

1. Sign in to SideCar.
2. Open **Profile**.
3. Select **Delete account**.
4. Review the deletion information.
5. Enter `DELETE` and confirm.

For security, SideCar may ask the user to sign out and sign in again before
deletion. Users must finish or cancel open rides and wait for pending refunds
or payouts before the account can be deleted.

### Request deletion without app access

Send a request to `hi@ride-sidecar.com` using the email address associated with
the SideCar account. Include “SideCar account deletion” in the subject. SideCar
may ask for information needed to verify account ownership before processing
the request.

### Data deleted or de-identified

After a valid deletion request, SideCar deletes or de-identifies the user's:

- authentication account and active sessions;
- name, email address, profile photo, and profile details;
- verification and insurance files stored under the user's account;
- user-specific database collections; and
- ride-linked conversation content.

### Data that may be retained

Limited ride, booking, payment, refund, payout, dispute, safety, and audit
records may be retained in de-identified or legally required form for fraud
prevention, accounting, tax, dispute resolution, safety, regulatory compliance,
and the rights of other users. Retention depends on the record type and legal
obligation.

For questions, contact `hi@ride-sidecar.com`.
